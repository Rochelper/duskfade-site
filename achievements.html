Achievement Guide: All 25 (1000G)

Duskfade has 25 achievements totaling 1000G: 12 Bronze, 5 Silver, 8 Gold, and 1 Platinum. Here's the complete list with tips for earning each one.

Platinum: "Master Clockmaker"

Earn all trophies in Duskfade. This platinum requires completing every other achievement — a true completionist run. Expected playtime for 100%: 20-25 hours.

Achievement Roadmap

Phase 1: Story Playthrough (Natural Unlocks)

During your first playthrough, you'll naturally unlock:

  • Sign me an autograph (find Arpeggio)
  • We fixed Time, didn't we? (break Tick Town's Time Anchor)
  • Back to school (find Clockmaking Academy)
  • Be my muse! (help Tailord escape)
  • You won't be alone anymore (befriend N1-C0)
  • The last star (help Grandpa cross the Veil)
  • Don't talk to strangers (meet Desert Explorer)
  • I've missed you (meet Cuco)
  • Despair, get ready (open Tower door)
  • Don't leave my side (escape Tower with Allira)

Phase 2: Exploration & Collectibles

After finishing the story, backtrack with all abilities to collect:

  • Cat army (all kittens)
  • Master of melodies (all music boxes)
  • Collector (all cards)
  • Don't call Dr. Peppermint (all health upgrades)
  • Advanced clockmaking (all energy upgrades)
  • The most beautiful sword in the kingdom (all rainbow ingots)
  • Compulsive buyer (all Traveler purchases)
  • No peeking! (all Wally encounters)
  • The mask of pain (best outfit for Palette)

Phase 3: Misc & Secret Achievements

  • Master XII's secret (Grandpa's big secret)
  • Not that way! (fall from Tower top)
  • You seem familiar (cloudie with strange crown)
  • You have no heart (break Cloudie Guard's pots)
  • You really didn't see it coming? (kill Wilson)
  • A grandmother's love (Canyon Granny)

Phase 4: Platinum

Master Clockmaker (Platinum)

Once all 24 other achievements are unlocked, the platinum pops automatically. You are the Master Clockmaker!
