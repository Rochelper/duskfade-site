How to Beat Solitude in Duskfade

Solitude is the seventh mandatory boss, waiting at Clockmaker’s Rest. The fight is about ignoring the noise — the cloned echoes — and locking onto the real core.

Solitude — Overview

7th Boss · Clockmaker’s Rest

Solitude is the seventh of Duskfade’s nine mandatory boss fights, found at Clockmaker’s Rest near the endgame. The theme is isolation — Solitude surrounds you with copies so you can never be sure which shape is real. The arena is a hanging-clock workshop with grapple points strung between the gears.

The core trick is misdirection. The clones hit, but only the core takes damage. Chasing every echo wastes your openings; the discipline is to thin them with slow-time (the ability) and strike the core during the silence window it opens.

Attacks & How to Counter

Echo Swarm

Several clones attack from every angle. Use slow-time to read the real one (the core glows faintly) and punish the core, not the mirrors.

Isolation Grip

Solitude grabs and drags you into a silence field (no abilities). Wriggle free with a time-freeze dodge — do not panic-swing inside the field.

Clock Strike

Hanging clocks drop from above. Grapple away, then drop for a falling hit on the exposed core.

Phase Strategy

Phase 1 (100% – ~66% HP)

Two echoes spawn. Focus the core — a quick slow-time makes the real shape readable, and you land your hits on it while the mirrors whiff. Ignore the clones entirely unless one is mid-swing next to you.

Phase 2 (~66% – ~33% HP)

Four echoes plus the silence fields. Slow-time is now survival, not just a read — pop it the moment the field forms, then Heavy the core during the brief silence break when Solitude is vulnerable.

Phase 3 (~33% – 0% HP)

The core shields between strikes. Do not over-commit; hit only on the open window, then grapple out before the next swarm. Patience through the noise is the whole fight.

Key Tips for Solitude
  • Attack the core, not the echoes. Mirror damage is fake — only the faint-glow core counts.
  • Slow-time is your lens. It makes the real shape readable and buys the window to strike.
  • Don’t fight the silence field. Time-freeze dodge out, then reposition.
  • Grapple the clock drops. They open the core; use them instead of dodging randomly.

Recommended Approach

  • Keep Solitude in view and treat every extra shape as a decoy until slow-time confirms the core.
  • Use short Heavy combos on the core after a silence break — never a long light flurry.
  • Grapple between the hanging clocks to dodge the Clock Strike and set up falling hits.
  • The time-freeze dodge slips the Isolation Grip — pair it with an immediate reposition.
  • 2 hits per window, then move — the swarm punishes anyone who lingers.
