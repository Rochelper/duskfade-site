2. Analytics — Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic (which pages are read, approximate region, device type, referral source). It does not collect your name, email, or payment details. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes this data under Google's privacy practices. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tools or browser add-ons.

This helps us measure content quality; it carries no personal identifier and is recorded by Google Analytics alongside other analytics events.
