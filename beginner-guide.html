Everything to Know Before You Start

Duskfade seems like a cute clockwork platformer, but it hides surprising depth. The demo trips people up because it barely teaches its most important mechanic. Here's everything you need to know before wielding the Minutero.

1. The Time-Freeze Dodge (Most Important!)

This is the #1 mechanic most players miss

A well-timed dodge roll stops time. When you read an enemy's swing and roll into it at the last possible moment — just before the hit connects — the world freezes for 1-2 seconds while Zirian keeps moving. You get a window of free hits on a frozen enemy.

The Common Mistake

In most action games, your instinct is to roll the instant an attack telegraphs. In Duskfade, that's exactly wrong. Rolling early does nothing special — you just get a normal dodge with i-frames.

The fix: patience. Let the wind-up nearly connect. Watch the enemy's swing arc reach its apex and begin descending toward you. THEN roll. When the freeze triggers, you'll know — the enemy hangs mid-lunge, projectiles stop in the air, and Zirian is free to swing.

How to Practice

The basic ink enemies in the opening forest area are perfect practice dummies. Their attacks are slow and heavily telegraphed. Let one wind up, count "one Mississippi," then roll. Once you feel the freeze trigger once, you'll never forget the timing.

Once this clicks, the entire game gets easier. The Wrath boss fight goes from a wall to a rhythm. Every boss in the game is built around this mechanic.

2. Your Sword: The Minutero

Zirian's weapon is the Minutero — a giant clock hand that belonged to his grandfather. It's both a weapon and a tool.

Combat Basics

ActionHowNotes
Basic ComboAttack button x3Wide sweeping strikes that flow one into the next. Third hit has the most knockback.
Projectile DeflectTimed swing at incoming projectileBat enemy projectiles back at them. Crucial vs ranged enemies.
Special AttacksWhen energy is chargedConsumes energy. Normal attacks restore energy.
TransformationUnlocks as you progressMinutero picks up new forms and attack patterns deeper in the game.

No Block Button

Duskfade has no dedicated block. Your defense is entirely dodge-based. This means you can't passively tank hits — you must read attacks and react. The time-freeze dodge is your shield.

3. Movement is Everything

The platforming is where Duskfade shows its pedigree (Jak & Daxter DNA). Here are the movement techniques the demo never spells out:

Essential Movement Tech

TechniqueHowWhy It Matters
Double JumpJump twiceCovers an absurd amount of horizontal ground.
Jump Roll ExtensionDodge roll at the tail end of your second jumpStretches the gap significantly. Clears "how do I reach that?" ledges.
Grapple Hook-shotUse Grapple at anchor pointsZips you between hook points. Part of traversal AND boss fights.
Target SwapSwitch lock-on mid-comboCombined with constant jumping, keeps you out of the pile-on.
Wall JumpJump at wall, then jump againFor vertical traversal in temple areas.
Rail GrindingLand on railsFor mine shaft and crystal cave sections.
GlidingAfter peak of jumpCovers long gaps, especially in cloud regions.
Standing Still = Death

In combat, standing still in a crowd is how Zirian dies. Always be jumping, dodging, and swapping targets. The movement system is designed to flow from traversal directly into combat and back — use it.

4. Cuckoo: Your Skill Tree Companion

The little clockwork bird following Zirian is Cuckoo — a sassy mechanical companion that the story implies Allira built. Cuckoo isn't just cosmetic — the bird feeds a set of support skills you unlock through skill trees.

As you earn Upgrade Gears and collectibles, you pour points into four skill tree branches:

BranchWhat It UpgradesPriority
MinuteroSword combos, transformations, special attacksHigh

5. Progression: No XP, Just Gears

Duskfade doesn't use a traditional XP/level system. Instead, you collect resources and spend them:

ResourceHow to GetWhat It Does
Upgrade GearsFrom enemies, chests, and secretsSpend on skill trees (4 branches)
Clock Heart PiecesCollect 3 pieces+1 max health (achievement: "Don't call Dr. Peppermint")
Star ShardsSmash boxes, defeat enemiesCurrency for purchases
Rainbow IngotsHidden in each regionSword upgrades (achievement: "The most beautiful sword in the kingdom")
Music BoxesHidden collectiblesReturn to Arpeggio (achievement: "Master of melodies")
Metroidvania Structure

The worlds loop back on themselves. You'll see doors and paths you can't reach early on — they open once you've got the right gadget or ability. This means it pays to poke at the edges of every zone before moving on. Backtracking with new tools reveals hidden secrets.

6. Bonfire Checkpoints

Duskfade has bonfire checkpoints — a souls-like system. Resting at a bonfire:

  • ✓ Restores full health
  • ✓ Refills healing potions
  • ✓ Serves as respawn point on death
  • ✓ Likely serves as upgrade station
Respawn

Enemies in cleared areas likely respawn when you rest at a bonfire (standard souls-like behavior). Use this for farming Upgrade Gears and Star Shards.

7. Tips Before You Begin

Tip 1: Play the Demo First

A free demo is available right now on Steam and PS5 — about 1-2 hours of content covering Tick Town outskirts, the magical forest, ancient clocktower ruins, and the Wrath boss fight. Demo progress doesn't carry over, but the muscle memory does.

Tip 2: Use a Controller

The developers recommend playing with a controller. Full support for Xbox and PlayStation controllers on all platforms. The timing-based dodge and platforming precision benefit greatly from analog sticks.

Tip 3: Explore Everything

Each region is designed as a single, complete area — not broken into small chunks. Look for alternative paths, hidden alcoves, and routes that seem unreachable. Many require abilities you get later — make a mental note and come back.

Tip 4: Smash Everything

Boxes, containers, and breakable objects drop Star Shards (currency) and sometimes Upgrade Gears. The Minutero opens chests by swinging at them — no key needed. When you see a locked chest, just attack it.

Tip 5: Don't Rush Bosses

Bosses in Duskfade are movement tests, not slugfests. Rushing in and trading hits gets you flattened. Read their patterns, use the environment (grapple points, platforms), and layer the time-freeze dodge for telegraphed swings. Patience wins.

8. NPCs You'll Meet

Cuckoo

Mechanical bird companion. Built by Allira. Feeds support skill tree.

Allira

Zirian's sister. Trapped in the Clock Tower by Despair. The driving force of the story.

Arpeggio

Butterfly-like musician. Gives you the Grapple hook-shot. Collect music boxes for him.

Tailord

NPC who needs help. Find all their kittens scattered across the world.

N1-C0

Robot NPC. Befriend them for the "You won't be alone anymore" achievement.

Dr. Peppermint

Health upgrade NPC. "Don't call Dr. Peppermint" = max out your health.

Grandpa

Zirian's grandfather, former owner of the Minutero. Help him cross "the Veil."

Cloudies

Friendly, fluffy inhabitants of Tick Town. Some guard pots (don't break them!).

Wally

Hidden character. Find him every time for the "No peeking!" achievement.

Palette

Fashion NPC. Dazzle them with your best outfit for an achievement.

The Traveler

Desert merchant. Buy all their items and upgrades for an achievement.

Canyon Granny

Generous grandmother in the canyon area. Gives you a little money.
