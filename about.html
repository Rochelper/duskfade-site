About This Guide

Duskfade Guide is a fan-made resource dedicated to helping players master the clockpunk world of Duskfade.

Our Mission

This guide was created to provide comprehensive, easy-to-follow resources for players of Duskfade — the 3D action platformer from Weird Beluga Studio and Fireshine Games. We cover everything from beginner tips to advanced boss strategies, collectible locations, and achievement hunting.

Whether you're a newcomer trying to understand the time-freeze dodge or a completionist hunting for every Rainbow Ingot, we've got you covered.
