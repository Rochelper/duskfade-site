Collectibles Guide

Duskfade hides 10 distinct collectible types across the clockwork world of Tearfeld. None of them are missable — you can finish the story first, then sweep each region with the traversal abilities you have unlocked. This guide covers what each one does, the trophy it ties to, and where to start looking.

Metroidvania cleanup tip

Several collectibles sit behind abilities you earn late in the story (hook-shot, grapple, glide, wall-grab). Rather than backtracking constantly on your first run, clear the campaign, then revisit each region one by one. Some Music Boxes are also handed to you automatically through story progression.

Music Boxes

There are 17 Music Boxes. Return them all to Arpeggio — the butterfly-like NPC who speaks in rhyme, first met in the Starstone Mines and later found at the inn in Tick Town. Each box is locked behind a Simon Says light puzzle. Note: the trophy only pops after you have beaten the game, reloaded your save, and spoken to Arpeggio, because some songs unlock through story progression.

Achievement: Master of Melody

Find all 17 Music Boxes. Regions containing one: Tick Town, Erazus Forest, Scorched Grove, Starstone Mines, Mount Cinder, Meridian Village, Lantern Port, Apprentice's Wing, Great Library, Canal Hamlet, Celestial Kingdom, Royal Highroads, Auric Palace, Deepthorne, Stonetusk Canyon, Clockmaker's Rest, Desert of the Last Memory.

N1-C0ins

N1-C0ins are antique coins scattered through the hub and every region. Collect them all and exchange them for the Card collection (the Collector trophy). They are an easy pickup to miss because they are often tucked along side paths branching off fast-travel points.

Achievement: Collector

Gather every N1-C0in and trade them in for the full card set.

Thieving Kittens

Thieving Kittens are Tailord's lost kittens, hidden across Tearfeld. Catching them all unlocks new outfits — and the Come Here Right Meow trophy. They are small and easy to miss; invest in Cuckoo's Item Finder support skill to ping nearby hidden objects.

Achievement: Come Here Right Meow

Find every one of Tailord's kittens.

Upgrade Gears & Crystals

Upgrade Gears are the most common pickup — dropped by enemies, found in chests, and awarded for platforming challenges. Spend them (together with Crystals) on the four skill-tree branches: Minutero, Zirian, Gadgets, and Cuckoo. Read each upgrade's description before spending — a respec has not been confirmed.

Achievement: Shopaholic

Buy every upgrade available from the shop using Gears and Crystals.

Farming Gears

Enemies respawn at rest points. Clear a nearby group, rest, repeat — the soulslike way to top up your Gears.

Clock Heart Pieces

Collect 3 Clock Heart Pieces to raise your maximum health by 1. They are the most survival-critical collectible — prioritize them before the Wrath fight, since that boss punishes a missed dodge fast. Look in side paths, on raised platforms, and behind routes that open up after the hook-shot.

Achievement: A Clock Heart Piece a Day...

Find every Clock Heart Piece to max out your health.

Rainbow Ingots

Rainbow Ingots are the Minutero's upgrade material. Find them all to max out the sword and unlock its ultimate form — one of the highest-value trophies. They hide in the most obscure corners of each region, and a few are even sold at the shop next to the Tick Town fast-travel point.

Achievement: The Most Beautiful Sword in the Land

Collect every Rainbow Ingot.

Star Core Fragments

Star Core Fragments increase your maximum Time Energy — the resource behind special attacks like the Blast Attack and Chronoblast. Like Clock Heart Pieces, they sit off the main path and behind platforming challenges.

Achievement: Advanced Clockmaking

Max out your Time Energy.

Flasks of Dye & Echoes

Flasks of Dye unlock alternate color schemes for Zirian — cosmetic only, no trophy attached. Echoes are story and world-information pickups surfaced in a dedicated menu; they deepen the lore but are not required for completion. Keep both when you find them; you will want them for a true 100%.

Broken Stained Glass

The Broken Stained Glass pieces are the hardest collectibles in the game — far more obscure than anything else, requiring you to turn over every stone across each region. Together they reveal one of Duskfade's biggest secrets (Master XII's Secret).

Achievement: Master XII's Secret

Recover every Broken Stained Glass piece.

Wally — the Hidden Character

Wally is a nod to "Where's Wally?" — a tiny figure hiding in crowd scenes and background details. You must spot him every time he appears.

Achievement: No Peeking!

Find Wally in every one of his appearances.

Where to Sweep — Region Routes

Collectible routes span every area. The main sweeps are: Tick Town, Tick Town Volcano Path, Erazus Forest, Scorched Grove, Starstone Mines, Mount Cinder, Sea Path, Meridian Village, Lantern Port, Clockwork Academy (Apprentice's Wing, Craftsman's Wing, Great Library, Scriptorium of the Twelve), Hills Path, Canal Hamlet, Celestial Kingdom (Royal Highroads, Auric Palace), Desert Path (Deepthorne, Stonetusk Canyon, Clockmaker's Rest, Desert of the Last Memory), and Chronos Aeternum. Prison of the Beast and the Veiled Chambers have no collectibles.

Completionist Checklist

  • Return all 17 Music Boxes to Arpeggio (Master of Melody)
  • Collect every N1-C0in and trade for the Card set (Collector)
  • Catch all of Tailord's Thieving Kittens (Come Here Right Meow)
  • Buy every upgrade with Gears + Crystals (Shopaholic)
  • Max health with Clock Heart Pieces (A Clock Heart Piece a Day...)
  • Max the Minutero with Rainbow Ingots (The Most Beautiful Sword in the Land)
  • Max Time Energy with Star Core Fragments (Advanced Clockmaking)
  • Recover all Broken Stained Glass (Master XII's Secret)
  • Find Wally every time (No Peeking!)
  • Collect Flasks of Dye and Echoes for a true 100%
