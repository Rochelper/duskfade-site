Who Is Zirian?

Zirian is the player character of Duskfade — a young workshop apprentice who sets out to rescue his sister Allira from the Clock Tower that has plunged the world of Tearfeld into eternal night. He is not a chosen hero; he is a brother who refuses to give up.

Zirian at a Glance

Role

The protagonist and only playable character. You fight, explore, and grow as Zirian across four corrupted regions.

Weapon

The Minutero — his grandfather’s clock-hand sword, a Clockmaker tool repurposed as a weapon with multiple transformable forms.

Companion

Cuckoo, the sassy mechanical bird implied to have been built by Allira. It fights beside him with its own support skill tree.

Look

Brave but inexperienced. His rat-tail hairstyle is a deliberate nod to Disney’s Treasure Planet (2002), one of the game’s tonal inspirations.

Zirian’s Role in the Story

When their clockmaker grandfather passed away, Zirian and his younger sister Allira were left with his workshop and his secrets. Allira’s investigation into the Master Clockmakers accidentally unleashed the Inkies and trapped her inside the giant Clock Tower sealed with four chains. Zirian’s entire journey — smashing each chain, defeating each emotional boss, and mastering the clockwork world — is driven by one goal: bring Allira home.

Unlike a typical “chosen one,” Zirian starts with basic sword skills and unlocks abilities, gadgets, and Minutero transformations as he journeys through the corrupted regions. His growth from apprentice to hero is the spine of the story.

Real-World Heart

The narrative was shaped by Narrative Designer Ricardo Chorques Mesa, drawing from his real loss of a grandfather who was also a clockmaker. Zirian’s grief and determination are the emotional core of Duskfade.

Zirian’s Skill Tree

Progress in Duskfade is item-driven: find Upgrade Gears and spend them across the game’s four skill-tree branches. The Zirian branch is the protagonist’s own progression, alongside the Minutero, Gadgets, and Cuckoo branches.

  • Zirian branch — core combat upgrades: sword combos, dodge windows, and survivability.
  • Minutero branch — new sword forms and transformations, including the ultimate form unlocked with Rainbow Ingots.
  • Gadgets branch — traversal tools like the hook-shot and grapple that double as boss-phase mechanics.
  • Cuckoo branch — support abilities for the mechanical companion.
Cosmetic Only

Flasks of Dye unlock alternate color schemes for Zirian. They are purely cosmetic — no trophy is tied to them, so you can safely ignore them on a trophy-only run.

Combat Kit & The Time-Freeze Dodge

Zirian’s bread-and-butter is the time-freeze dodge — a perfectly-timed roll that briefly stops time, leaving frozen enemies open to free hits. It is the single skill that turns every boss from a wall into a rhythm, and the one to master first.

Melee

Short sword chains with the Minutero. Use 2–3 hits after a safe opening, then reposition — most bosses punish greed.

Dodge

The time-freeze roll is your best defense and offense. Layer it onto telegraphed swings.

Gadgets

Hook-shot, grapple, glide, and wall-grab open new routes and break boss rhythms in phases.

Before tough fights, collect Clock Heart Pieces (3 = +1 Max Health) and raise Time Energy with Star Core Fragments. See the Beginner Guide to learn the dodge step by step.

Voice & Cast

Duskfade features fully voice-acted cinematic cutscenes, with Zirian and Cuckoo’s banter performed by a voice cast the developers have praised. As of launch, Weird Beluga has not published an official character voice-actor credits list, so we are not listing names we cannot verify. We will update this section if cast details are released.

Zirian — FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions players have about the protagonist of Duskfade.

Who is Zirian in Duskfade?

Zirian is the young apprentice protagonist of Duskfade. He is a trainee clockmaker who sets out to rescue his younger sister Allira from the Clock Tower after it drags the world of Tearfeld into an endless night. He wields the Minutero, a sword that doubles as a transformation device, and is accompanied by his mechanical bird companion Cuckoo (built by Allira).

What is Zirian's signature mechanic?

The time-freeze dodge. A perfect dodge at the last moment freezes time briefly, giving Zirian a window to land free hits. It is the core combat loop and the foundation of every successful boss fight.

What is the Minutero?

The Minutero is Zirian's sword and transformation device. Its skill tree adds combo extensions, special-attack power, new transformation forms, and (via the bat-swing) the ability to reflect enemy projectiles back at them.

Does Zirian have a level or XP system?

No. Zirian does not gain XP from kills. His power comes from (1) skill-tree investments, (2) Minutero transformations, (3) gadget unlocks like the Grapple and Blast Attack, and (4) collectible upgrades such as Clock Heart Pieces and Rainbow Ingots. Thorough exploration is mandatory.

What is Zirian's design inspired by?

Zirian's design has been highlighted by reviewers for its Treasure Planet / Star Trek Prodigy-influenced aesthetic - a young hero with a confident smile, a sturdy utility coat, and clockpunk goggles - and his sister Allira was created by Weird Beluga with a deliberately treasure-hunting-adventurer look.

How does Cuckoo help Zirian?

Cuckoo is the mechanical bird Allira built for Zirian. It has its own support skill tree: combat assist, distraction, minor healing, Time Extension (which extends Zirian's time-freeze window), and Item Finder (which pings hidden collectibles). The deeper the bond, the more abilities Cuckoo unlocks.
