What's in the Demo? (5 Areas)

The demo was significantly expanded in June 2026 for Steam Next Fest. It now includes 50% more content than the original September 2025 release.

1. Tutorial Section

A short opening that teaches the core mechanics: moving, jumping, basic sword combat, and the all-important time-freeze dodge.

~10 minutes

2. Tick Town (Outskirts)

A clockpunk town area. Meet NPCs, find collectibles, and learn platforming basics. Talk to everyone — side characters drop useful lore and hints.

~15 minutes

3. Magical Forest

An ethereal forest filled with ink-like enemies. Practice the time-freeze dodge against slow, telegraphed attackers. Look for hidden Clock Heart Pieces.

~15 minutes

4. Ancient Temple / Clocktower Ruins

Platforming-heavy zone with wall jumps, rails, and grapple points. Here you unlock the Grapple and Blast Attack abilities.

~20 minutes

5. Mines (Added in June Update)

New area added in the Steam Next Fest update. Features rail-grinding sections and tougher enemies. Leads directly to the boss arena.

~10 minutes

Total Demo Length

60-90 minutes for a thorough playthrough. Speedrunners can finish in ~40 min; completionists chasing every collectible may spend 2 hours.

Wrath Boss Strategy (Demo Finale)

The demo ends with a boss fight against Wrath — a hulking machine with gear-blade arms and chains trailing from its back.

The Read

Wrath is a movement test, not a slugfest. It has two attack modes:

  • Up close: Lunges and overhead slams — high damage, short range
  • At range: Lobs projectile shots — moderate damage, tracks slowly

The Strategy

Do NOT face-tank Wrath

Trying to trade hits with Wrath gets you flattened. Its damage output far exceeds yours at demo level. You must use the arena.

  1. Use the Grapple — Wrath winds up charged attacks that you interrupt by grappling to platforms around the arena. Break its rhythm.
  2. Time-freeze dodge — When Wrath does telegraphed swings, roll at the last moment to freeze time and get free hits.
  3. Punish recovery — After Wrath misses a big attack, close in for 2-3 sword hits, then retreat. Don't get greedy.

Demo Tips for Beginners

Common Mistakes to Avoid

MistakeWhy It's BadFix
Rolling too earlyYou lose the time-freeze window — the best tool in the gameWait until the swing is about to connect, THEN roll
Cutting your double jump shortYou miss ledges that look unreachableAdd a dodge roll at the end of your second jump for extra distance
Blitzing to the exitYou leave behind upgrades that make later areas easierExplore side paths — the world folds back on itself
Ignoring CuckooYou miss the companion skill treeCheck Cuckoo's upgrade menu — it has its own progression
Forgetting to open chestsYou miss Clock Heart Pieces (health upgrades)Swing the Minutero at every chest — it's your master key

What to Collect in the Demo

  • Clock Heart Pieces — Increase max health (3 pieces = 1 heart)
  • Rainbow Ingots — Currency for Minutero upgrades
  • Upgrade Gears — Skill points for the ability tree

After the Demo: Should You Buy?

Buy If...

  • You enjoyed PS2-era platformers (Jak & Daxter, Kingdom Hearts, Ratchet & Clank)
  • The time-freeze dodge mechanic clicked for you
  • You love exploration and finding hidden collectibles
  • You want a 15-20 hour single-player campaign

Wait If...

  • You found the combat too simple or clunky
  • You prefer souls-like difficulty (Duskfade is more accessible)
  • You want multiplayer (Duskfade is single-player only)
  • You're on Xbox — no demo available, try Steam first
