The Minutero: Clock-Hand Sword

Zirian's weapon is the Minutero — a giant clock hand that belonged to his grandfather. It's a weapon, a key, and a transformable tool that grows with you throughout the adventure.

Kingdom Hearts Connection

The Minutero is intentionally designed to evoke the Keyblade from Kingdom Hearts — same handle and guard design, same "weapon as key" concept. Weird Beluga cites KH as a primary inspiration, and the Minutero is their love letter to it.

Energy Management

The energy economy is the core of Duskfade's combat depth. Normal attacks restore energy, special attacks consume it. This means you can't just spam specials — you need to weave normal attacks in between to keep your energy topped up. The rhythm is: normal combo → dodge read → time-freeze → free hits → special when energy is full → repeat.

Upgrades: Rainbow Ingots

Rainbow Ingots are the primary sword upgrade material. They're hidden in each region of Tearfeld — find them all to max out the Minutero's potential.

Achievement: "The most beautiful sword in the kingdom"

Find all rainbow ingots across all regions. This is a 90G Gold achievement — one of the highest-value collectible achievements in the game. Don't miss any!

Exploration Tip

Rainbow Ingots are often in areas that seem unreachable when you first visit a region. They're designed to be found on return visits with new abilities. Make a mental note of suspicious spots and come back when you have the Grapple, double jump upgrades, or other gadgets.

The Minutero as Key

One of the Minutero's most useful features: it's also your master key. When you spot a locked chest off the main path, you already have what opens it. Just swing at the chest.

No Key Hunting

Unlike many platformers that require finding specific key items for specific chests, Duskfade keeps it simple. The Minutero opens every chest. This means: when you see a chest, open it immediately. Don't wait — come back later only if you can't reach it due to missing abilities.

The Golden Rule

Don't stand still after a combo. Always end with a dodge roll, jump, or grapple. The game punishes stationary play heavily — enemies swarm and surround you. Make "attack → reposition" your muscle memory.

Maxing Out the Minutero

To get the most powerful version of the Minutero, you need to collect:

  • All Rainbow Ingots — Sword upgrades (achievement: "The most beautiful sword in the kingdom", 90G Gold)
  • Upgrade Gears — Spend on Minutero skill tree branch for new combos and transformations
  • Traveler's items — Some upgrades may be purchasable from the Traveler NPC (achievement: "Compulsive buyer", 80G Gold)
Grandpa's Secret

There's a secret achievement (90G Gold) for discovering "Grandpa's big secret" — likely tied to the Minutero's ultimate form or backstory. Keep an eye out for lore revelations about Master XII.
