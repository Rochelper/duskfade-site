How to Beat Anchored Wilhelm in Duskfade

Anchored Wilhelm is the eighth mandatory boss, fought in Chronos Aeternum just before the Clock Tower. The whole fight is a race to break his Time Anchors before they freeze you in place.

Anchored Wilhelm — Overview

8th Boss · Chronos Aeternum

Anchored Wilhelm is the eighth of Duskfade’s nine mandatory boss fights — the last gate before the Clock Tower and the Despair finale. The theme is time itself anchoring you. The arena is a crystalline time-rift with anchor points you must destroy to stay mobile.

Wilhelm plants Time Anchors that root you where you stand. The fight is a loop: destroy the anchors (grapple in, Heavy them) while dodging his beam, then punish the core during the unfreeze window. Let the anchors pile up and you are a sitting target.

Attacks & How to Counter

Time Anchor

Wilhelm drops anchors that root you. Grapple to a free point, then Heavy the anchor to shatter it before it locks your movement.

Freeze Beam

A beam that locks movement in a line. Time-freeze dodge through it, then hit the core — the dodge also breaks the freeze on you.

Chrono Slam

A slam that creates a slow-field. Stay mobile on the grapple rails and punish on recovery, never on the active field.

Phase Strategy

Phase 1 (100% – ~66% HP)

Two anchors at a time. Prioritize destroying them the moment they appear — a rooted Zirian cannot dodge the Freeze Beam. Once clear, land a short Heavy on the core.

Phase 2 (~66% – ~33% HP)

Four anchors plus the beam. Use slow-time to manage the field, and Heavy anchors in pairs. The core only opens after the anchors are down, so anchor-clearing is literally your damage phase.

Phase 3 (~33% – 0% HP)

Wilhelm enrages and anchors respawn fast. Do not try to clear them all — break the one nearest you, slip the beam, hit the core on its open window, then move. Greed on anchor-clearing gets you frozen.

Key Tips for Anchored Wilhelm
  • Anchors first, always. A rooted Zirian cannot dodge — clearing them is survival, not optional.
  • Grapple to a free point. Never try to walk out of an anchor; grapple breaks the line.
  • Heavy the anchor. Light hits are too slow — one committed Heavy shatters it.
  • Time-freeze the beam. The dodge both dodges and unfreezes you.

Recommended Approach

  • Keep Wilhelm in view and treat every new anchor as the top priority until it is shattered.
  • Grapple between rails to stay mobile — the slow-fields punish anyone grounded.
  • Use short Heavy combos on the core after the anchors fall — never a long flurry.
  • The time-freeze dodge is the release valve for both the beam and the root.
  • 2 hits per window, then move — the respawn cadence in Phase 3 is relentless.
