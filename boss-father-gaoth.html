How to Beat Father Gaoth in Duskfade

Father Gaoth is the sixth mandatory boss, fought in the Veiled Chambers beneath the Auric Palace. The whole fight turns on one rule: Heavy attacks — light hits do nothing to his armor.

Father Gaoth — Overview

6th Boss · Veiled Chambers (Auric Palace)

Father Gaoth is the sixth of Duskfade’s nine mandatory boss fights, reached in the Veiled Chambers beneath the Auric Palace late in the campaign. He reads as a regal, armored patriarch — the weight of inherited expectation. His arena is a mirrored chamber with pillars you can use for height and line-of-sight breaks.

His armor shrugs off light attacks; only Heavy attacks (the slow, glowing swings) break his guard and open the real damage window. The shield rule that governed The Watcher applies here too — bring your upgraded Minutero and commit to the heavy combo.

Attacks & How to Counter

Armored Charge

Gaoth rushes the frontal arc. Live on the flanks and rear; grapple behind a pillar to force a whiff, then swing.

Shield Slam

He raises a barrier and slams. Heavy attack the shield during recovery to stagger him and open the core.

Crown Shockwave

A radial blast. Jump or grapple to high ground, then drop for a falling Heavy on the exposed crown.

Phase Strategy

Phase 1 (100% – ~60% HP)

Gaoth mixes Armored Charge with Shield Slam. Bait the charge to a flank, punish the rear, and when the shield rises, land one committed Heavy to stagger him. Light attacks here are a waste of the opening.

Phase 2 (~60% – ~30% HP)

The chamber begins to collapse — pillars fall and the floor hazards open. Use the remaining pillars for height, and time your Heavy on the shield exactly when he rears for the slam. This is where most of your damage comes from.

Phase 3 (~30% – 0% HP)

Gaoth enrages with faster charges. Never trade hits — one or two Heavies per opening, then reposition behind cover. Patience wins; greed gets you caught in the Crown Shockwave.

Key Tips for Father Gaoth
  • Heavy, not light. Light attacks do nothing to the armor — every opening should be a Heavy combo.
  • Use the pillars. Height breaks his line of sight and sets up falling Heavies.
  • Bait the charge. Make him commit to the frontal rush, then grapple behind for free damage.
  • Stagger on the shield raise. That is the only reliable stagger window in the fight.

Recommended Approach

  • Keep Gaoth facing away by living on his flanks — the frontal arc is the deadliest zone.
  • When the barrier rises, commit to a single Heavy rather than a light flurry.
  • Grapple to pillar tops to dodge Crown Shockwave and drop Heavies on the crown.
  • The time-freeze dodge still helps you slip the charge — pair it with a reposition.
  • 2 Heavies per opening, then move — do not linger after a stagger ends.
