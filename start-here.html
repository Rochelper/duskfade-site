Start Here: Your First Playthrough Roadmap

Duskfade drops you into a clockpunk world wrapped in eternal night. The game does a good job teaching basics, but it never tells you what order to tackle things. This page is the map. Follow the five steps below — each one links to a deeper guide when you need it.

If You Only Do One Thing

Practice the Time-Freeze Dodge First

Most bosses have attacks that are nearly impossible to dodge any other way. Spend your first 20 minutes in the tutorial forest learning the timing — roll as the swing is about to connect, not before. Once it clicks, the whole game opens up.

Start Here FAQ

Should I play the demo first?

Recommended. It's free on Steam and PS5 and covers the opening hour. Progress doesn't carry to the full game, so treat it as a tutorial, then start fresh.

What order should I do the regions?

Take the four chains in the order the story hands them to you. Start with the Volcano Path (Ezorus Forest → Mount Cinder → Prison of the Beast): it ends with Wrath and Guayota, and Mount Cinder is where Arpeggio gives you the Hookshot — the single most important item in the game. Then the Sea Path, the Academy Path and the Sky Path. After every chain, go back through earlier regions: the Hookshot, Stopwatch, Sundial and Crunia’s Wings each open routes that were dead ends on your first visit. See the full walkthrough for the region-by-region route.

Is Duskfade hard?

No — it's an accessible action-platformer, closer to Jak & Daxter than Dark Souls. The challenge is exploration and timing, not punishing combat.

How long to 100%?

Main story 10–15 hours, full completion 15–20 hours, all achievements 9–12 hours of focused play. See How Long to Beat.
