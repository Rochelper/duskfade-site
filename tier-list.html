Tier List: Everything Ranked

Our rankings for abilities, gadgets, bosses, and collectible priorities. Built from demo data, gameplay footage, and developer coverage. Updated after launch!

Abilities & Movement Tier List

S
  • Time-Freeze Dodge — The #1 mechanic. Extending the freeze window is the highest-priority skill tree upgrade. Without this, the game is significantly harder.
  • Grapple (Hook-Shot) — Essential for traversal AND boss fights. Unlocks new areas, enables combat mobility, and is required for Metroidvania backtracking.
A
  • Double Jump — Core platforming ability. Covers absurd distance, especially with roll extension.
  • Jump Roll Extension — Hidden tech: dodge roll at the end of double jump stretches gap coverage. Many "unreachable" ledges become reachable.
  • Target Swap — Essential for crowd control. Switch enemies mid-combo while leaping. Without this, groups overwhelm you.
  • Combo Extensions — More hits = more energy restored + more damage per combo string.
B
  • Blast Attack — Solid damage + knockback repositioning. Good for crowds and creating space.
  • Rail Grinding — Niche but necessary for specific areas (Mount Cinder mines).
  • Wall Jump — Required for vertical sections but not universally useful.
  • Projectile Deflect — Situational but satisfying. Bats enemy shots back for bonus damage.
C
  • Chronoblast ("Shotgun") — Ranged option in a melee game. Nice to have but not essential. Energy cost makes it impractical for spamming.
  • Glide — Useful for specific platforming but limited combat application.
  • Flip — Stylish dodge with i-frames, but the time-freeze dodge is strictly better.
  • Air Dash — Extra mobility, but jump roll extension often suffices.

Gadgets Tier List

S
  • Grapple — The undisputed king of gadgets. Traversal, combat, Metroidvania progression — it does everything. Get it ASAP from Arpeggio.
  • Teleport Pads — Fast travel is essential for backtracking. Always activate new pads.
A
  • Blast Attack — Your bread-and-butter special. Damage + knockback + crowd control. Energy-efficient.
B
  • Chronoblast — Decent ranged option but energy cost limits usefulness. Good for specific enemy types.
C
  • Minutero Transformations (late) — Not bad, but the base form + Blast Attack handles most situations. Late transformations are more about style than necessity.

Boss Difficulty Tier List

S
  • Despair (Final Boss) — The ultimate test. Multi-phase fight in the Clock Tower. Tests every mechanic. Expected to be the hardest fight in the game.
A
  • Fear (Underwater Realm) — Water mechanics add complexity. Hard to judge until launch, but underwater bosses are notoriously tricky.
  • Jealousy (Sun-scorched Dunes) — Desert hazards + likely enrage mechanics. Expected to be challenging.
B
  • Sorrow (Ethereal Forest) — Unique mechanic (eyes + cage). Once you know the flow — survive, hit eyes, run to cage — it's a 1-2 minute fight. Conceptually interesting but mechanically simple.
  • Wrath (Mount Cinder) — First boss, fair difficulty. Teaches you the time-freeze dodge. Becomes easy once the dodge clicks. A good "tutorial boss."

Collectible Priority Tier List

Which collectibles should you prioritize? Here's our ranking by impact on your playthrough:

S
  • Clock Heart Pieces (Health) — More health = more survivability = easier everything. Always prioritize these.
  • Upgrade Gears — Your skill tree currency. Every gear makes you stronger. Grab them whenever you see them.
  • Rainbow Ingots (Sword) — Minutero upgrades increase your damage output significantly. Find them in each region.
A
  • Time Energy Upgrades — More energy = more special attacks. Synergizes with Blast Attack and Chronoblast.
  • Star Shards (Currency) — Needed for Traveler purchases (Compulsive buyer achievement). Don't waste on consumables.
B
  • Music Boxes — Arpeggio quest. 80G achievement. Collect as you explore naturally.
  • Tailord's Kittens — Cat army quest. 80G achievement. Invest in Item Finder skill.
  • Cards — Collection completion. 80G achievement. Check every chest and enemy.
C
  • Outfits/Cosmetics — Only needed for the "mask of pain" achievement (15G). Low priority.
  • Wally Encounters — 15G achievement but missable. Look carefully but don't stress.
  • Multicoloured Flasks — Side quest items. Low impact on gameplay.

NPC Importance Tier List

S
  • Arpeggio — Gives you the Grapple (essential gadget) AND accepts Music Boxes (80G achievement). The most important NPC by far.
  • Dr. Peppermint — Health upgrade NPC. Visit regularly to turn Clock Heart Pieces into max health increases.
  • The Traveler — Sells items and upgrades. Buy everything for the Compulsive buyer achievement (80G).
A
  • Tailord — Kittens quest (80G). Also has story significance (escape Academy, 15G).
  • Cuckoo — Your companion. Not a traditional NPC but the skill tree branch is important.
  • Grandpa / Master XII — Story significance (help cross the Veil, 30G) + secret (90G Gold).
B
  • N1-C0 — Befriend for 15G. Robot NPC, likely in Underwater Realm.
  • Palette — Fashion NPC. Show best outfit for 15G.
  • Canyon Granny — Gives money. 15G achievement.
  • Desert Explorer / Traveler — Meet for 15G + buy items for 80G.
C
  • Wally — Hidden character. Find every time for 15G. Easy to miss.
  • Cloudie Guard — Break their pots for 15G. Minor NPC.
  • Wilson — Kill for 15G. Details TBD.
  • Cloudie with strange crown — Confront for 15G. Likely in Cloudy Heights.
