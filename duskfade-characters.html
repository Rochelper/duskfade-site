Main Characters

Zirian

Role: Protagonist (Playable)

Identity: Young workshop apprentice

Weapon: Minutero (grandfather's clock-hand sword)

Companion: Cuckoo (mechanical bird)

Personality: Brave but inexperienced. Not a chosen hero — just a brother who refuses to give up on his sister. His rat-tail hairstyle is a nod to Disney's Treasure Planet (2002), one of the game's tonal inspirations.

Motivation: Rescue his sister Allira from the Clock Tower and restore the fabric of time that has plunged the world into eternal night.

Zirian starts with basic sword skills and unlocks abilities, gadgets, and Minutero transformations as he journeys through four corrupted regions.

Read Zirian’s full character guide →

Allira

Role: Damsel / Emotional Anchor

Identity: Zirian's younger sister

Status: Imprisoned in the Clock Tower by Despair

Personality: Kind, curious, and deeply bonded with Zirian. Their shared memories of their grandfather (a clockmaker) are woven into the world's puzzles and lore.

Role in Story: Allira is not just a rescue objective — she represents the personal stakes of Zirian's grief. The Clock Tower feeds on memories of loss, and Allira's presence there connects to the siblings' past.

The story is shaped by Narrative Designer Ricardo Chorques Mesa's real experience of losing his grandfather, making Allira's capture a metaphor for holding onto those we've lost.

Read Allira’s full character guide →

Cuckoo

Role: Companion (Mechanical Bird)

Identity: Mechanical cuckoo bird, origin tied to Master Clockmakers

Function: Travel companion, puzzle solver, and combat support

Personality: Sassy, loyal, and surprisingly expressive for a machine. Think Daxter meets Navi — but actually helpful.

Gameplay: Cuckoo has its own skill tree separate from Zirian's. Upgrading Cuckoo unlocks:

  • Combat assists (distraction, targeted strikes)
  • Puzzle interactions (activating clockwork mechanisms)
  • Exploration aids (scent tracking for hidden items)

Cuckoo is the emotional heart of the game — a mechanical creature that develops something resembling genuine attachment to Zirian.

Palette

Role: Fashion NPC (Tick Town)

Function: Cosmetic outfits, dyeing, and the “The mask of pain” achievement

Personality: Stylish and exacting — only your most dazzling look will impress her.

Palette runs the game’s outfit system. Collect cosmetic outfits, dye them with Flasks of Dye, and show your best look to earn the 15G achievement.

How the Characters Connect

Relationship Map
  • Zirian & Allira — siblings. Their shared memories of their clockmaker grandfather are woven into the world's puzzles and lore.
  • Allira → Cuckoo — the sassy clockwork bird is implied to have been built by Allira, so she is present on the journey even while imprisoned.
  • Zirian & Cuckoo — travel companions; Cuckoo has its own skill tree separate from Zirian's.
  • Despair → Allira — the final force holding Allira captive in the Clock Tower; it knows more about Allira and Cuckoo than it lets on.
  • Arpeggio — a tall, butterfly-like figure who gives Zirian the hook-shot on the way to Mount Cinder.

The world of Tearfeld is populated by friendly Cloudies (fluffy cloud-like inhabitants of Tick Town) and threatened by Inkies, the ink-like monsters Allira's investigation accidentally unleashed.

Duskfade features fully voice-acted cinematic cutscenes, with Zirian and Cuckoo's banter and NPC conversations performed by a voice cast the developers have praised for landing the duo's personality traits. The Master Clockmakers

Who Are They?

The Master Clockmakers are the mysterious ancient beings who built the world of Duskfade. They created the Clock Tower, the Minutero, and the clockpunk technology that permeates every region. By the time Zirian's journey begins, they have long since vanished — leaving behind their creations, their secrets, and the broken fabric of time.

Throughout the game, Zirian uncovers fragments of the Clockmakers' history:

  • The Clock Tower — Their greatest creation, now corrupted by Despair
  • The Four Regions — Each shaped by a different Clockmaker's design philosophy
  • The Minutero — A clockmaker's tool repurposed as a weapon
  • Cuckoo — Likely a Clockmaker creation, though its exact origin is mysterious
  • The Chains — Four chains locking the Clock Tower, each connected to a region's boss

The Clockmakers' story parallels themes of legacy, creation, and the responsibility of those who build powerful things. Their absence is what allowed Despair to take root.

Design Philosophy

The developers at Weird Beluga have stated: "Overcoming the absence of a loved one is not about forgetting; it's about learning to walk accompanied by their memory." Each boss fight is designed to teach Zirian (and the player) a different lesson about processing loss.

Supporting NPCs

Tailord

Role: Village Tailor / Quest Giver

Location: Tick Town

A friendly tailor who gives Zirian side quests. Most notably, Tailord has lost several kittens and asks Zirian to find them — a collectible quest spanning the entire game.

Finding all of Tailord's kittens unlocks a special achievement and a cosmetic reward.

Wally

Role: Hidden NPC / Collectible

Location: Various (hiding spots across all regions)

A shy character who hides in hard-to-reach spots throughout the world. Finding Wally in every region is tied to an achievement.

Look behind waterfalls, under bridges, and atop high platforms.

Canyon Granny

Role: Friendly NPC

Location: Canyon area

A generous grandmother who gives Zirian a small amount of money and lore about the Master Clockmakers. Talk to her every time you visit — she has new dialogue after each boss.

Townsfolk of Tick Town

Role: Background NPCs

Location: Tick Town

Various residents who provide lore hints, side quest breadcrumbs, and flavor dialogue. Talk to everyone — some hint at hidden collectibles in their region.

Behind the Scenes: The Real Story

Inspiration

Duskfade's narrative was shaped by Ricardo Chorques Mesa, Narrative Designer at Weird Beluga. The game's themes of grief, memory, and growth are drawn from his real experience of losing his grandfather — a clockmaker in real life.

This personal connection is why every boss fight feels emotionally weighted rather than just mechanically challenging. The clockpunk aesthetic isn't just a visual choice — it's a metaphor for the time we have with loved ones and the memories we carry forward.

Weird Beluga Studio is a Spanish indie team based in Montreal/Spain. Their debut, Clid the Snail (2021), won awards through Sony's PlayStation Talents program. Duskfade is their second game, published by Fireshine Games.

Duskfade Characters FAQ

The most common questions about the cast and story of Duskfade:

Who is the main character in Duskfade?

The protagonist is Zirian, a young workshop apprentice who wields the Minutero — his grandfather's clock-hand sword. He is the only playable character; you guide him across four corrupted regions to rescue his sister.

Who is Zirian's sister?

Allira is Zirian's younger sister, imprisoned in the Clock Tower by the force of Despair. Rescuing her is the emotional core of the story — and the Clock Tower feeds on memories of loss, tying her fate to the siblings' past.

What is Cuckoo in Duskfade?

Cuckoo is Zirian's mechanical companion bird — sassy, loyal, and surprisingly expressive. It has its own skill tree (combat assists, puzzle interactions, scent tracking for hidden items) and is implied to have been built by Allira herself.

How many bosses are in Duskfade?

There are five emotional bosses, each representing a stage of grief: Wrath, Sorrow, Fear, Jealousy, and the final boss Despair. They are not just enemies — they are manifestations of loss Zirian must overcome.

Who is the final boss of Duskfade?

The final boss is Despair, the force holding Allira captive in the Clock Tower. It knows more about Allira and Cuckoo than it lets on, and overcoming it is the climax of Zirian's journey.

Is Duskfade fully voice acted?

Duskfade features fully voice-acted cinematic cutscenes, with Zirian and Cuckoo's banter performed by a praised voice cast. Regular dialogue and NPC conversations are text-based. The game ships with 6 supported languages (English full audio + subtitles).

Who made Duskfade?

Duskfade is developed by Weird Beluga, a Spanish indie team, and published by Fireshine Games. Their debut was Clid the Snail (2021). The narrative was shaped by designer Ricardo Chorques Mesa, drawing on his real experience of losing his clockmaker grandfather.

What is the story of Duskfade about?

At its heart, Duskfade is about grief, memory, and growth. The Clock Tower has plunged the world into eternal night, and Zirian must learn that overcoming the absence of a loved one is not about forgetting — it's about learning to walk accompanied by their memory.
