How to Beat Guayota in Duskfade

Guayota is the final boss of the Volcano Path, fought in the circular arena inside the Prison of the Beast. Unlike the humanoid Wrath, Guayota is a huge quadrupedal creature with a steam engine bolted to its back and a full set of area-of-effect attacks. This is the fight that breaks the first chain sealing the Clock Tower.

The One Rule

Never stand directly in front of Guayota. Almost every attack in its kit covers the frontal arc — live on its flanks and rear, and only step in during a recovery window.

The Pillar Phase (Every 25% HP)

Every time Guayota loses 25% of its health, the arena changes:

  1. Guayota moves to the centre of the arena and starts jumping in place.
  2. Pillars fall from above, giving you a vertical route.
  3. Lava floods the arena. Before it rises, climb to the highest pillar.
  4. Look up at the walls — chains hang around the arena.
  5. Grapple and pull every chain you can reach. Pulling them all drops the lava level.
  6. Drop back down and resume the normal flank-and-punish loop.
Read the Reset

The single most reliable tell: when Guayota walks to the centre, stop attacking. Players who get onto the pillars early have a far calmer lava-drain sequence than players who try to squeeze in one more combo.

The Winning Loop

Guayota is a movement-heavy boss. You win by surviving the arena hazards as much as by landing hits. The fight runs on a repeating cycle:

  • Take a side angle. Start on the left or right flank and use short melee strings — never long combos.
  • Leave the pink/red danger zones. Roll out of the telegraphed circles before the stomp or blast lands.
  • Climb when it floods. Centre move → platforms → grapple chains → floor again.
  • Punish the recoveries. Wall-crash knockdowns, slams and post-slam recharges are your real damage windows.
  • Reset away from the front. Once the floor is safe, run clear of the face and rebuild your side position.

Before You Enter

Buy every combat upgrade you want before committing to the boss route. You cannot return to the Tinker Stall until Guayota is dead, so do your shopping in advance.

Guayota — Rewards

  • A second refillable potion slot — you can now carry two potions at once.
  • Another memory featuring the three family members (Zirian, Allira and their grandfather).
  • Most importantly: the victory lets you break the first chain locking the tower where your sister is held.

Guayota — FAQ

Where is Guayota fought?

In the circular arena inside the Prison of the Beast, at the end of the Volcano Path.

How do I stop the lava from flooding the arena?

Climb the tallest pillar, look up at the walls, and grapple every chain you can reach. Pulling all of them drains the lava so you can return to the ground.

What is Guayota's biggest punish window?

When its charge hits the arena wall and it collapses — you get several seconds of free damage on the recovery.

Should I attack Guayota's head?

No. Aim for the body. The head is where the bite hitbox lives and it rewards punishing greedy players.

How often does the pillar phase happen?

Every time Guayota loses 25% of its health — three times per fight by design.

Is Guayota harder than Wrath?

Yes. Wrath tests your positioning; Guayota adds arena hazards, a lava cycle and a grapple route on top. See the Wrath guide for the earlier fight.
