About Duskfade

Duskfade is a 3D action-platformer soulslike developed by Weird Beluga, released on August 13, 2026. Set in a clockpunk world where time itself has fractured, players take on the role of Zirian, a young hero who must save Allira from the corrupted Clock Tower.

The game's defining mechanic is the time-freeze dodge — a precision-based ability that lets players briefly halt time to slip past deadly attacks. Mastering this single mechanic is the difference between struggling through every encounter and clearing bosses in a handful of attempts.

For New Players

Don't skip the time-freeze tutorial. It's the single most important skill in the game, and most bosses have attacks that are nearly impossible to dodge without it. Practice on the Sorrow boss first — its slow, telegraphed attacks are perfect for learning the timing.
