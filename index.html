About Duskfade

Duskfade is a 3D action-platformer developed by Weird Beluga and published by Fireshine Games, released on August 13, 2026 on Steam. Master a seamless blend of dynamic platforming and fast-paced combat — jump, dash, grapple, and glide through thrilling challenges that rekindle the magic of classic action platformer favourites.

Explore stunning realms shaped by the Master Clockmakers, each filled with mystery, magic, breathtaking vistas, and endearing characters. Navigate ethereal forests, underwater realms, sunlit dunes and cloudy heights — the world of Duskfade is alive and teems with secrets for you to uncover.

Experience a powerful coming-of-age journey filled with heart, loss, and the courage to face the unknown. Join a cast of charming characters to face down long forgotten foes, each embodying an emotional hurdle.

For New Players

Don't skip the time-freeze tutorial. It's the single most important skill in the game, and most bosses have attacks that are nearly impossible to dodge without it. Practice on the Sorrow boss first — its slow, telegraphed attacks are perfect for learning the timing.
