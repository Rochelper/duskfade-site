# Duskfade Guide & Wiki

A fan-made, source-checked guide site for **Duskfade** — the clockpunk 3D action-platformer by **Weird Beluga** (published by Fireshine Games). Out now on Steam, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2.

🌐 **Live site:** https://www.duskfade-guide.com

## What's inside

- **Start Here** — a 5-step new-player roadmap (first hour → final boss)
- **Boss Strategies** — phase-by-phase counters for all 5 main bosses: **Wrath** (Howling Geode) → **Guayota** (Prison of the Beast) → **Sadness** → **The Watcher** → **Despair** (Clock Tower)
- **Full Walkthrough** — four path-based routes (Volcano / Sea / Academy / Sky) with chain-breaking order
- **Abilities & Skill Trees** — every gadget, Cuckoo support skill, and the time-freeze dodge
- **Combat Guide** — stamina economy, combo strings, projectile deflects
- **Collectibles & Achievements** — every hidden item and all 25 Steam achievements (PS5: 26 with Master Clockmaker Platinum)
- **100% Roadmap** — efficient region-by-region sweep with common-miss callouts
- **Lore & World** (Tearfeld), **News & Updates**, **Endings**, **Secrets**, **How Long to Beat**, **Weapons & Tier List**
