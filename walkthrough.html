Walkthrough: Demo & Full Game

The demo covers the opening hour — Tick Town outskirts, a magical forest, ancient clocktower ruins, and the Wrath boss. Here's a step-by-step guide, plus the full game region structure for when the game launches August 13.

Game Structure Overview

Duskfade is structured around four regions, each containing a chain that locks the Clock Tower. You must travel to all four and shatter the chains to gain entry to the tower and face Despair.

Region Design

Each area is designed as a single, complete region — not broken up into small chunks. You can look around and discover the way forward, alternative paths, and well-hidden secrets. The regions loop back on themselves Metroidvania-style, with routes you can only reach using abilities gained later.

Demo Walkthrough (Part 1: Tutorial)

1. The Opening

The game opens with a cinematic cutscene: Zirian returns home to his sister Allira with sweet treats for them to enjoy. Before the explosion, Allira seems obsessed with helping their grandfather (who passed away). The scene establishes the characters, the relationship, and the stakes. The art style and voice acting are reminiscent of Pixar or DreamWorks.

Pick Up the Minutero

After the explosion, Zirian regains consciousness. Grab the conspicuous clock hand stuck in the nearby ground. This is the Minutero — once belonging to your grandfather. It's both your weapon and your key for the entire game.

2. Tutorial Area — Basic Mechanics

The tutorial introduces core mechanics:

  • Basic Combat: Three-hit combo chains, target-swap
  • Projectile Deflect: Bat enemy projectiles with timed swings
  • Double Jump: Cover large gaps
  • Dodge Roll: Your primary defensive tool (remember: time-freeze triggers on last-second rolls!)
  • Chest Unlocking: Swing the Minutero at locked chests — no key needed
  • Star Shards: Smash boxes and defeat ink creatures for currency
  • Healing Potions: Find refills in the world

3. Meet Cuckoo

The tutorial area ends when you meet Cuckoo — a small, sassy clockwork bird the story implies was built by Allira. Cuckoo becomes your permanent companion, feeding the support skill tree throughout the game.

Cuckoo is Your Skill Tree

Don't think of Cuckoo as just a mascot. The bird feeds a set of support skills you unlock through trees. As you earn Upgrade Gears and collectibles, pour points into Cuckoo alongside Zirian's own abilities.

Demo Walkthrough (Part 2: Tick Town)

4. Tick Town — Hub World

After the tutorial, you arrive in Tick Town — or at least a fractured version of it. This serves as the game's hub world. Key features:

  • Cloudies: Friendly, fluffy inhabitants. Talk to them for worldbuilding and clues.
  • Clock Tower: A giant, imposing structure has appeared, wreathed in everlasting night. Huge chains lock down the tower.
  • Teleport Pads: Unlock these for fast travel. Always activate a new pad when you find one.
  • Blocked Areas: Many sections are blocked off in the demo — accessible in the full game after gaining certain upgrades.
  • Time Anchor: Break Tick Town's Time Anchor (achievement: "We fixed Time, didn't we?")
Explore Everything in Tick Town

Talk to every NPC. Break every box. Open every chest. Many Cloudies offer brief conversations that provide worldbuilding clues. Some hint at collectible locations or side quests (Tailord's kittens, Arpeggio's music boxes).

Demo Walkthrough (Part 3: Magical Forest)

6. The Magical Forest

From Tick Town, you venture into an enchanted forest alive with danger and mystery. This is where the platforming depth kicks in:

Platforming Challenges

  • Moving platforms: Clock-gear themed, rotating and shifting
  • Pendulum swings: Large swinging obstacles — time your passage
  • Rotating platforms: Similar to obstacle course shows — jump on, ride, jump off
  • Hook points: (After getting Grapple) zip between anchor points
  • Wall jump sections: Vertical climbing in narrow passages

Enemies

Strange, inky creatures appear across the forest. They burst with a satisfying splat after a few hits. Combat is fast-paced — Zirian can switch between enemies on the fly during combos, leaping across areas to smack targets. But enemies don't wait around and will quickly surround you. Liberal use of jumping and dodging is needed.

Practice the Time-Freeze Dodge Here

The forest enemies are the perfect practice dummies for the time-freeze dodge. Their attacks are slow and heavily telegraphed. Let one wind up, wait for the swing to almost reach you, then roll. Once you feel the freeze trigger once, the whole game clicks.

Collectibles in the Forest

  • Star Shards: From boxes and enemies
  • Upgrade Gears: Hidden in chests and off the main path
  • Clock Heart Pieces: Look for hard-to-reach spots
  • Music Boxes: Hidden for Arpeggio's quest

Demo Walkthrough (Part 4: Ancient Clocktower Ruins)

7. Descend into the Ruins

The demo's third area is an ancient clockpunk temple where time itself feels broken. This is where the game's clockpunk aesthetic truly shines — gears, mechanisms, and time-distortion effects fill the environment.

New Abilities

In the ruins, you unlock:

  • Blast Attack: High-damage cone hit. Use to break cracked walls and clear groups of enemies. The knockback repositions you tactically.
  • Grapple (Hook-Shot): From Arpeggio in the caves. Zip to anchor points. Immediately becomes part of both traversal and the upcoming boss fight.

Ruins Challenges

  • Time-distortion puzzles (void-like echoes reveal worldbuilding clues)
  • Crystal formations blocking paths — use Blast Attack
  • Grapple anchor sequences — chain zip across chasms
  • Gear-rotation puzzles — interact with mechanisms
Void Echoes

Throughout the ruins, you'll find void-like echoes that reveal interesting worldbuilding and narrative clues. They envelop Zirian and Cuckoo in a cosmic bubble while active — a form of time distortion. Don't skip these — they deepen the story.

Demo Walkthrough (Part 5: Wrath Boss Fight)

8. Mount Cinder — Wrath Arena

After traversing the crystal cave system, you reach the Wrath boss arena — a wide platform surrounded by a deep drop into flowing lava below. A short cutscene sets the stage.

Wrath is an imposing sight: large gear segments attached to its arms as blades, and chains protruding from its back. It looks more like a Kingdom Hearts Heartless than any other foe you've encountered.

Boss Strategy Summary

Treat Wrath as a movement test, not a slugfest. It lunges and slams up close, then lobs ranged shots when you back off. Face-tanking gets you flattened. Use the grapple to reach platforms and break Wrath's rhythm on charged attacks. Layer the time-freeze dodge for its telegraphed swings. Close in for damage during recovery windows.

For the full phase-by-phase Wrath strategy, see our Boss Guide.

Demo Notes

The demo takes about 90-120 minutes to complete. Progress does NOT carry over to the full game. The demo has been holding around 91% positive on Steam across hundreds of reviews — a strong sign the feel lands for most players.

Full Game Walkthrough (Post-Launch)

Coming August 13

The full game releases August 13, 2026. Below is the expected structure based on all available information. Full detailed walkthroughs for each region will be added after launch.

Region 1: Mount Cinder (Volcanic)

  • Boss: Wrath
  • Theme: Volcanic, crystal caves, lava
  • Key NPC: Arpeggio (gives Grapple)
  • Key Ability: Grapple (Hook-Shot)
  • Collectibles: Rainbow Ingots, Clock Heart Pieces
  • Exploration: Crystal cave system, rail grinding sections, grapple chains

Region 2: Ethereal Forest

  • Boss: Sorrow
  • Theme: Magical forest, enchanted
  • Key Mechanic: Eyes + cage trap boss fight
  • Collectibles: Music Boxes (for Arpeggio), Tailord's kittens
  • Exploration: Dense forest, platforming challenges, hidden alcoves

Region 3: Underwater Realm

  • Boss: Fear
  • Theme: Sunken city, water
  • Key NPC: N1-C0 (befriend for achievement)
  • Expected Mechanics: Underwater traversal, swimming/gliding, water-level changes
  • Collectibles: Rainbow Ingots, Clock Heart Pieces

Region 4: Sun-scorched Dunes

  • Boss: Jealousy
  • Theme: Desert, canyon
  • Key NPCs: Desert Explorer ("Don't talk to strangers"), Canyon Granny
  • Key NPC: The Traveler (buy all items for achievement)
  • Expected Mechanics: Heat hazards, sand, canyon platforming
  • Collectibles: Rainbow Ingots, Clock Heart Pieces, Wally encounters

Optional: Cloudy Heights

  • Theme: Sky, floating islands, clouds
  • Key NPC: Cloudie with strange crown ("You seem familiar" achievement)
  • Expected Mechanics: Gliding, air dash, floating platforms
  • Notes: May be optional or contain endgame secrets

Final: Clock Tower

  • Boss: Despair (Final)
  • Theme: Time distortion, gears, chains
  • Story Beats: Open the Tower door, find Allira, escape with her
  • Key NPC: Grandpa (help him cross "the Veil")
  • Secret: Master XII's Secret (90G Gold achievement)
  • Fall Achievement: "Not that way!" — fall from the top of the Tower

Chain Breaking Order

The four chains locking the Clock Tower can likely be tackled in a flexible order (similar to Jak & Daxter's precursor satellite missions). However, some regions may require abilities from earlier regions. Recommended order based on ability progression:

Backtrack After Each Chain

After breaking each chain, return to Tick Town and previous regions with your new abilities. Many previously inaccessible areas will now be reachable, containing Rainbow Ingots, Clock Heart Pieces, and other collectibles.
