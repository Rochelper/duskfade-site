Duskfade is out now. Full launch on Steam (PC), PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2. A 20% launch discount drops the price to $23.99 and runs for two weeks (ends August 27). Current Version & Performance

The launch build is version 1.0.3. Performance notes from launch coverage:

  • PC: Uncapped framerate.
  • Xbox Series X: Targets 4K / 60 fps with VRR and HDR (some dips reported in the Tick Town hub).
  • Nintendo Switch 2: 60 fps docked, 40 fps handheld (with VRR).
  • PlayStation 5: 60 fps target, HDR supported.

Weird Beluga has indicated interest in adding post-launch frame-rate and resolution options, so a future patch is plausible — we will note it here if announced.

News & Updates — FAQ

Is Duskfade out yet?

Yes. Duskfade released on August 13, 2026 for PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2.

Is there a discount right now?

A 20% launch discount ran for two weeks after release (through August 27, 2026). Check the Steam store page for the current price, as regional pricing and promotions differ.

What platforms is it on?

PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2 — all available digitally at launch.

Is there still a demo?

The pre-launch demo introduced Tick Town and the opening regions. Availability of a post-launch demo varies by platform — the full game is the recommended way to play now.

Will there be DLC or expansions?

Nothing has been officially announced. We will update this page if Weird Beluga or Fireshine confirm post-launch content.

How long is the game?

Story completion is roughly 10–15 hours, with full 100% completion (collectibles, Time Rifts, the stained-glass secret) landing around 15–20 hours. See How Long to Beat.

What language support does it have?

The game shipped with full interface and subtitle localization, including Traditional Chinese, making it accessible to a broad audience at launch.
