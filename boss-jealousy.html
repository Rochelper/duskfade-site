How to Beat Jealousy in Duskfade

Jealousy is the fourth of the five emotional bosses. It is the last gate before the finale, and the one most obsessed with holding on — guarding what it has, stealing what you reach for, and twisting the fear of loss into a weapon. Here is what we know and how to approach it.

Jealousy — Overview

Fourth Boss · Sun-scorched Dunes

Jealousy is the fourth of the five emotional bosses. It appears in the Sun-scorched Dunes, the blistered desert region of Tearfeld. Jealousy represents envy — the bitter fear that someone else will take what you love, or that you will lose what you already hold.

Where Fear works on your nerve and Sorrow slows you with grief, Jealousy works on your grip. Early read-throughs of the design suggest an arena built around guarding, stealing, and contested objectives — the boss may snatch Zirian’s items or block the path to Allira, forcing you to fight for what is yours. The fight is rated Hard — the rituals you drilled on the first three bosses are what let you hold your ground here.

Jealousy — Phases & Attack Patterns

Jealousy is built around guarding and grasping. In the Sun-scorched Dunes it steals what you reach for and blocks the path to Allira, forcing you to fight for what is yours. The time-freeze dodge is your tool for reclaiming stolen ground — freeze the arena, take it back, then strike.

Phase 1 — The Hoard

  • Snatch — Jealousy rips away your active gadget (often the grapple) and uses it against you. Freeze time to interrupt the steal, then grapple it back during the recovery.
  • Sand Veil — a blinding sweep that hides the boss’s next move. Back off to safe range and wait it out; do not swing into the unknown.
Greed trap

Chasing the boss into its stolen loot is exactly how Jealousy punishes you. Land two or three safe hits, then reposition — never over-commit while it holds your gear.

Phase 2 — Mirror of Want

  • Borrowed Blade — Jealousy wields a copy of your own weapon, mimicking your combos. Bait the copied swing with a feint, then punish the slower real version.
  • Envy Spiral — a spinning projectile ring fired from the stolen gadgets. Time-freeze through the gap rather than rolling, since the ring has no blind spot on the ground.

Phase 3 — The Closing Fist

  • Grasp of Want — the arena shrinks as Jealousy claws the edges inward. Stay mobile on the remaining rails; a cornered Zirian gets grabbed.
  • Searing Claim — a final lunge that steals a chunk of health on contact. Save a Chronoblast to stagger it, then unload your remaining combo into the exposed core.
Community refinement

This phase map is based on early full-game clears and is still being tightened as more players reach Jealousy. Found a timing that works? Share it on the Boss Guide. For the fight just before it, see the Fear guide.

General Boss Strategy (All Five)

  • Master the time-freeze dodge first. A well-timed roll stops time briefly, giving you free hits on a frozen enemy.
  • Short chains over long combos. Most bosses punish players who keep attacking after a safe opening ends.
  • Use the environment. Hook-shot points and rails are not just traversal — they are part of several boss phases.
  • Upgrade before you fight. Spend Upgrade Gears on Zirian and Cuckoo, and collect Clock Heart Pieces (3 = +1 health) before tough fights.
